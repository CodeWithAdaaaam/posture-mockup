The Concept

A studio built
on intention
and the ocean.

POSTURE was born from a belief that movement should be precise, personal, and deeply felt. Nestled inside T Gallery Harhoura, our studio opens toward the Atlantic — the rhythm of the sea a constant companion to every session.

We offer two environments: a Reformer Pilates studio designed for transformation through resistance and alignment, and a private LED Light Therapy room for cellular recovery and radiance. Every detail is intentional — the light, the air, the silence between movements.

Our Offerings

Two rooms.
One practice.

Each space is designed to work on a different layer — the muscular, the cellular. Together, they form a complete wellness practice.

01

Reformer Pilates

Group & Private Sessions

Using Gratz-standard reformer machines, our sessions focus on precision, breath, and progressive strength. Whether in a small group or one-to-one, each session is adapted to your body and its current state.

  • Small group sessions — max 6 persons
  • Private 1:1 with certified coach
  • Beginner to advanced programming
  • Pre & postnatal specialization
  • Monthly progression tracking

02

LED Light Therapy

1-to-1 Private Sessions

Our private LED therapy room uses clinical-grade red and near-infrared wavelengths to accelerate cellular recovery, reduce inflammation, and enhance skin luminosity. Designed as both recovery and ritual.

  • Red light (630–660nm) + NIR (830nm)
  • Post-Pilates recovery protocols
  • Skin health & anti-inflammatory
  • 20–40 min session formats
  • Bundled packages available
